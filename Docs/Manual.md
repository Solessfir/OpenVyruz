# Player's manual

You fly the Vyruz, an armed hovercraft, up through the towers of the Untel Empire. Every level has one way out: find the lift and ride it to the next floor. Everything else in the building wants you dead.

## The basics

- **Shield and energy.** Damage comes off your shield first, then your energy. When energy runs out, the Vyruz blows up. You start the game with 5000 of each, both top out at 10000, and whatever you have left carries over to the next level.
- **Cash.** Destroyed enemies pay a reward, and gold lies around the levels. You spend cash on the map and on saving.
- **The lift.** Reach it to finish the level. Some lifts sit behind gates that need a key or a code.
- **Difficulty** changes how tough and alert the enemies are, and how much shield and energy you get. You can change it at any time in Options, even in the middle of a level.

## Controls

The ship turns toward the green sight under your mouse pointer; W and S thrust and back off, A and D turn.

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Fire | Left button | A or right trigger |
| Thrust forward / back off | W / S | Left stick |
| Turn | Move the mouse, or A / D | Left stick, or aim with the right stick |
| Strafe | Left Shift + A / D | Left stick, while aiming with the right stick |
| Next / previous weapon | Wheel down / up | RB / LB |
| Pick a weapon | 1 to 9 | |
| Low / high speed | Left Alt | How far you push the left stick |
| Map | Tab | B |
| Inventory | I | Y |
| Help | F1 | Hold Start |
| Name the panel's parts | F2 | Hold Back |
| Cycle the panel | ` (grave accent) | Tap Back |
| Pause | P | |
| Game menu | Esc | Tap Start |

Every binding but the pad's can be changed in Options; the wheel turned up takes the previous weapon while no binding uses it. Alt+Enter or F11 switches between fullscreen and a window.

With a pad (Xbox layout), the ship heads whichever way you push the left stick. Push the right stick to aim: the ship turns to face it and the left stick then moves and strafes around that aim, as in a twin-stick shooter. Aiming close to an enemy in view nudges the aim onto it. Without the right stick, firing does the same with the way you steer: an enemy close to it pulls the ship's heading onto it. Nothing locks on.

## Weapons

You have nine weapon slots. The gatling is armed from the start; the others need their ammo, which you find in packs. Pick up a pack and its weapon is ready to go. Keys 1 to 9 pick the weapons in the order below. Damage is per shot; a blast hurts less the further a target is from its centre.

| Weapon | Damage | What it does | Ammo pack |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gatling** | 2 x 100 | Twin machine guns. Fast and steady, but short range. You start with 1000 rounds. | Gun Pack, 250 rounds |
| **Grenade launcher** | 2000 | Grenades are lobbed a short way, hop along the ground and off walls, and go off with a big blast on hitting anything but terrain, or after three seconds. Great around corners. | Grenade Pack, 10 grenades |
| **Rocket launcher** | 2 x 500 | Two rockets at once, each with a small blast. | Rocket Pack, 30 rockets |
| **Flamethrower** | 400 | A stream of fire, 24 bursts a second, but very short range. Melts anything that gets close. | Fire Pack, 250 |
| **Saw launcher** | 3000 | Spinning blades that bounce around and hit hard. | Saw Pack, 30 blades |
| **Missile launcher** | 4500 | Homing missiles with a huge range and a big blast. Save them for tanks and walkers. | Missile Pack, 5 missiles |
| **Laser** | 3000 | A precise beam. Ammo is scarce. | Laser Pack, 10 shots |
| **Pulsar** | 1000 | Bouncing energy balls that hurt everything around them. | Pulsar Pack, 20 balls |
| **Mines** | 2000 | Drop them in an enemy's path. A mine arms after a few seconds and goes off with a big blast. | Mine Pack, 3 mines |

## Enemies

Enemies come in nine kinds. Within each kind, variants differ in weapons, toughness and habits: some patrol a set route, some wander, some hold a spot, and guardians always return to their post. Damage comes off an enemy's shield first, then its energy; where variants differ, the table gives the range. Most enemies slowly recharge their shield, so finish what you start.

| Enemy | Energy | Shield | What to expect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cops** | 300 | 300 - 1000 | Foot soldiers and the most common enemy. Weak but everywhere, with machine guns at first and rockets in the third tower. |
| **Turrets** | 500 - 1000 | 1000 - 2000 | Fixed gun posts in every level, from machine guns to missile and laser turrets. They can't move, so pick them off from beyond their reach. |
| **Spiders** | 500 | 4500 | Walking robots with a heavy shield and twin guns or lasers. They spot you from further away than anything else. |
| **Jeeps** | 600 | 300 - 800 | Fast patrol cars with rockets, and later missiles and stingers. |
| **Tanks** | 500 - 1000 | 2500 - 3000 | Slow and tough, with stinger rockets or missiles that hit very hard. |
| **Helicopters** | 200 | 3000 - 4000 | Gunships, and dragons in the third tower. Little energy but a big shield, armed with rockets, missiles, pulsars or stingers. |
| **Burners** | 300 | 1000 | Flamethrower bots. Harmless at range, deadly up close. |
| **Rollers** | 3000 | 5000 | Heavy rolling machines, among them the cutter, with grenades, saws or pulsars. Very tough. |
| **Walkers** | 1000 | 10000 | Giant robots with lasers that deal 3000 a hit. One kind is invisible. Bring missiles. |

Destroyed enemies often drop shield, ammo or a surprise.

**Generators** appear in the third tower. They keep spawning cops, jeeps, tanks, dragons or walkers until they reach their limit. Each one takes 5000 damage, and destroying it first saves a lot of trouble.

## Pickups

Drive over a pickup to take it. Crates, barrels and wrecks often hide one, so shoot them open.

| Pickup | Gives |
|---|---|
| **Shield** | +500 shield; the double gives 1000, the quadruple 2000 and the full one 10000 |
| **Repair** | +500 energy; the double gives 1000, the quadruple 2500 and the full one 10000 |
| **Gold** | +500 cash; the double gives 1000, the quadruple 2500 |
| **Ammo packs** | Ammo for one weapon (see [Weapons](#weapons)) |
| **Surprise** | A bonus or a malus, see below |
| **Saving plot** | Stay on it for about six seconds to save the game. Saving costs 100000 cash. |

## Surprises

A surprise kicks in half a second after you take it. Picking up another one ends the one in effect. A random surprise can be any of these.

**Bonuses**

| Surprise | Effect | Lasts |
|---|---|---|
| Invulnerability | Nothing can hurt you | 10 s |
| Invisible Drone | Enemies can't see or hurt you, but only mines fire, and lifts and gates won't let you through | 16 s |
| Touch & Destroy | Whatever you touch is destroyed | 8 s |
| Destroy Around | Everything near you is wrecked at once | instant |
| Circular Fire | Holding fire spins your shots all around you | 10 s |
| Triple Fire | Your shots fan out three at a time | 20 s |
| Time Warp | Everything but you moves at a quarter of its speed | 12 s |
| Auto Shield Up | Your shield recharges fast | 15 s |
| All Weapons Available | Every weapon fully loaded while it lasts | 15 s |

**Maluses**

| Surprise | Effect | Lasts |
|---|---|---|
| Trap | An explosion takes a quarter of your shield and energy | instant |
| No Light | Darkness all around you | 20 s |
| XTC | Double speed and a wild screen | 10 s |
| Weapon Jam | Your weapon won't fire | 10 s |
| Blocked Motor | You can't move or fire | 5 s |
| Thrust Loss | A quarter of your speed | 20 s |
| No Inertia Control | You slide around as if on ice | 15 s |
| Pinball Effect | You bounce off walls | 20 s |

One quirk of the original data: the reverse-commands malus is misspelt, so picking it up does nothing at all.

## Gates, keys and codes

- **Plain gates** slide open when anything comes near.
- **Numbered gates** need the key with their number. Keys 1 to 9 show up across the towers. Some gates use the key up, so you may need another for the next gate with that number.
- **Code gates** need a code that is scattered around the level one word at a time. Collect every word and the gate opens. The inventory (I) shows which words you still miss. In the last level, the code to the final lift runs against the clock.
- **Lifts** take you to the next level.

## The panel and the map

The panel shows your shield and energy, cash, ammo, speed, the radar, your position, kills, enemies left and the time. Tab cycles through smaller panels if you want more of the view; F2 names every part.

The radar shows enemies in red, keys and codes in yellow and lifts in blue. When a roof or wall hides your ship, a faint cyan outline shows its shape; enemies hidden the same way show a faint red outline.

The map (M) shows the whole level with you, the lifts and the saving plots on it. Opening it needs at least 21000 cash, and it keeps costing a little cash while it stays open.

## Tips

- Shoot crates and barrels: they hide shield, ammo and surprises. Mind the explosive barrels.
- Grenades and saws bounce, so you can hit turrets around corners without showing yourself.
- Keep missiles for walkers and tanks; their shields soak up everything else.
- Destroy generators before their spawns pile up.
- Save at saving plots once you can afford it; a later level is a long way to redo.
